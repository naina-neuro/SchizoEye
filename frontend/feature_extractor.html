SchizoEye Feature Extractor

Replays the three raw gaze CSVs (free viewing, SPEM, fixation+antisaccade) through the same 59-feature extraction pipeline as battery-controller.js — without re-running the actual test. Useful for checking the extraction math on a recording you already have.
