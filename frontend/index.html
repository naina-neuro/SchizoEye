Step 2: Calibration Guide

How to calibrate:

  1. A dot will appear at different positions around the screen.
  2. Look directly at the dot — keep your head still, move only your eyes.
  3. Press SPACE when you are looking at the dot to capture that point.
  4. The dot will move to the next position — repeat until all 33 points are captured.
  5. Sit at arm's length from the screen · Ensure your face is well-lit · Avoid bright light behind you.
